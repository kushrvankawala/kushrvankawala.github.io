project 01, in detailscroll to run the planner

HN LoRaWAN Planner Presented

Future City Hackathon 2026, HNVG challenge. Built by Team Odyssey, a team of four.

the problem

Heilbronn's water meters send their readings by radio, and an antenna on a rooftop has to catch them. Buildings and hills get in the way. With today's 8 antennas, about half of the homes are not heard and are still read by hand.

the plan

The city approved 71 sites for new antennas. Our planner picks the fewest of them that still hear almost every meter: 27 gateways reach 98% of the water meters.

the proof

Most homes are heard by two or more gateways, so one failure doesn't lose readings. New districts from the official building plans are included. We also planned Stuttgart from open data alone, 67 gateways, and presented it all with a live demo.
